# Sources

Based on [ECC search-first](https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC/blob/main/skills/search-first/SKILL.md), by Affaan Mustafa, under the MIT license. The upstream copyright and license are retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The upstream workflow, decision matrix, search categories, integration points, examples, and anti-patterns are retained. Adaptations include:

- Skill identity and explicit architecture scope, with brief external discovery even when a local implementation exists.
- On-demand channel checks and task-relevant MCP/skill searches.
- Optional, focused delegation with reuse of returned evidence.
- A borrowing option and selection by current requirements and total integration cost.
- Verification of decisive integration assumptions, including extension/composition paths.
- Question-driven stopping and concise delivery of the recommendation rather than the research history.
- Optional ecosystem integrations and a notice that examples require current verification.

The baseline is the upstream snapshot used in the local comparison, with SHA-256 `76835db48dd910061d2e4271b1151793d2fddbabeb2d2866eed068b3580c55d5`. The upstream link tracks the current project and may differ from this baseline.

No other skill instruction text is incorporated. ECC agents and companion skills are optional, not runtime dependencies.
